SOURCE POLICY
- Editable PNG generations are retained in assets/images/sources/.
- Production WebP files are the only versions imported by the application.
- Text, numbered steps, warnings, and factual labels stay in semantic HTML/SVG,
  not baked into generative images.

SHARED VISUAL RULES
- Purple #7A5AF8 is the brand accent, not the entire background.
- Supporting colors: teal, sky blue, amber, slate, and warm off-white.
- Indonesian people are represented naturally, respectfully, and without shame.
- No logos, government insignia, readable private data, or watermarks.
- Keep key subjects inside the safe center crop and faces away from text overlays.
- Use calm, practical safety imagery; avoid fear and victim-blaming.
- Production WebP should remain below 200 KB at the specified display dimensions.
