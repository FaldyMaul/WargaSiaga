WARGASIAGA MASTER WEB IMAGE STYLE

Intended product: a calm, trustworthy Indonesian public-safety web application that helps people recognize online scam patterns without shame or fear.

Global composition:
- Generate a clean production-ready web asset at the ratio named in the scene prompt.
- Keep the primary subject inside the central 78–82 percent safe area for responsive center cropping.
- Preserve generous breathing room and one clear focal point.
- Do not place important details near edges.

Visual system:
- Use restrained TailAdmin purple #7A5AF8 as a recognizable accent, never as a full dominant background.
- Support with deep navy #182230, teal #147D70, pale sky #EAF4FB, warm amber #D98B24, white, cool gray, and warm off-white.
- Lighting is soft, bright, and reassuring. Avoid fear-based drama.
- Materials and spaces should feel contemporary, practical, and recognizably Indonesian without clichés.

For human scenes:
- Hyperrealistic natural editorial photography of Indonesian people.
- Real skin texture, natural hair and fabric, believable hands, everyday environment, candid body language.
- Calm, thoughtful, empowered expressions; never depict the person as foolish or helpless.
- No celebrity resemblance and no identifiable private information.

For conceptual scenes:
- Premium corporate editorial 3D visualization with matte claymorphic geometry, soft ceramic or frosted-glass detail, gentle ambient shadows, and precise silhouettes.

Product constraints:
- No text is required inside the image. All meaningful copy and labels will be rendered as HTML/SVG.
- No real brands, platform logos, government insignia, watermarks, QR codes, or readable private data.
- No depiction that certifies a message, sender, account, or website as safe.
